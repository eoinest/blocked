## Reuse / print

- **Already owned:** ESP32-S2 Mini, soldering kit, 3D printer.
- **Check the drawer:** USB data cable that fits the Mac and the board's USB-C
  port, two short 28–30 AWG stranded wires, heat-shrink or insulating tape,
  and solder. Buy only what is missing.
- **Print:** [base](enclosure/stl/base.stl) + [lid](enclosure/stl/lid.stl), with the
  [fit coupon](enclosure/stl/fit-coupon.stl) first. Use gray/silver PLA or PETG.
  Paint and the dark trim are optional finishes.

Assembly details: [hardware](docs/hardware.md), [enclosure](docs/enclosure.md).
